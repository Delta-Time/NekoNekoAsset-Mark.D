# AI rules

- AI defaults OFF. Provider and model keys stay separately protected.
- User-selected text and explicit images may be sent to the selected provider.
  Thumbnail classification defaults OFF and normalizes selected images to at most
  1536 px. Never send cookies, asset archives or unrelated private records.
- AI never assigns, changes or removes a custom category, and never replaces a
  user's category (`../catalogue/AGENTS.md`).
