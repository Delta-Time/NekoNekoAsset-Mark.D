# Classification rules

- BOOTH sells unrelated goods; purchase or download access, or a removed page, does
  not establish VRChat relevance.
- BOOTH's own category decides before tags: `3D環境・ワールド` is World and
  `3Dモーション・アニメーション` Motion whatever a tag says. Shaders, Udon and gimmicks
  have no BOOTH category, so titles and tags separate them under tools, software,
  worlds or textures: Udon goes into the world SDK, Gimmick into the avatar SDK,
  World is an environment or a prop placed in one, and Shader includes particles
  and volumetric light placed in worlds. Outside BOOTH's 3D categories a word needs
  VRChat or Unity evidence, and bare アニメーション must not stand beside video or
  effect words.
- BOOTH's `3Dキャラクター` category makes a product Base whatever its tags say,
  unless software is declared. With no store category or only `3Dモデル（その他）`, a
  title that calls itself one named 3D model is enough; a removed page, add-on
  wording or a bare `3Dモデル` is not.
- Gumroad source classification also requires an explicit VRChat/VRC mention in
  the source title or visible product description; generic type or engine tags
  alone do not qualify. This gate does not apply to BOOTH or Jinxxy.
- Apply category rule changes to new sync observations; do not backfill existing
  classifications on open.
- Never assign, change or remove a custom category (`../catalogue/AGENTS.md`).
